    Within this folder is a collection of just about
    every static sprite from the mobile app, DOT, or
    rather Defender of Texel.   Every full character
    sprite can be found in the folder titled Sprites
    whereas just short of every character avatar can
    be located in the Avatars folder.

    The naming convention goes as such: [ABC-D]. The
    [ABC]  element cooresponds to the left column of
    the table below. In the right column is the name
    of the fighters,  organized alphabetically based
    on the first fighter in the evolution. The   [D]
    simply refers to each member of the evolution.
        
    Despite being Tier-2 fighters, Hinkypunk & Leep-
    a-Lil do not have an uncommon member.   For this
    reason, they are listed as fighters 001 and 002.
    Every other line has three members. 
    
        It should be noted that this naming
        convention does not apply to any
        folders within the Extras folder.

    Power fodder and dungeon bosses can be found in
    [\Extras\Unplayable].
